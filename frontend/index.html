StandIQ Operational User Guide

Guide for Government Procurement Officers, GeM Sellers, and SIH 2026 Technical Evaluators.

1. What is StandIQ?

StandIQ (SIH Problem Statement SIH26108) is an AI-powered Indian Standards recommendation engine designed for the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution. It eliminates technical errors, omissions of safety clauses, and citation of obsolete standards in public procurement tenders.

2. How does the Grounded Recommendation work?

StandIQ extracts key procurement entities (product category, materials, application, safety-critical clauses) from your tender specifications. It matches them against the official Bureau of Indian Standards repository using hybrid dense vector search and full-text keyword indexing. Every rationale quotes verified BIS scope text to guarantee 0% hallucination.

3. Generating Tender-Ready GeM Annexures

Click "Generate Tender Specification" on any recommendation to produce ready-to-copy technical compliance clauses. Use "Download Report (PDF)" to obtain an authentic, formatted evaluation report with normative cross-reference matrices.
